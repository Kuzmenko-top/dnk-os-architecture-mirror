# 🎨 DNK OS Canvas Studio — User Guide

Welcome to **DNK OS Canvas Studio**, the professional design and generative AI suite for e-commerce, advertising, and creative production.

---

## 🛠️ 1. Core Workflow: 5 Easy Steps

### Step 1: Upload Your Product
- Drag & drop your image directly onto the canvas or click **Upload Image** (`📷`) in the top toolbar.
- Move, scale, and rotate your image on the infinite canvas.

### Step 2: 1-Click AI Cutout (BiRefNet)
- Select your image node on the canvas.
- Click **✂️ 1-Click Cutout** in the toolbar.
- The high-precision AI will automatically remove the background and isolate your subject with pixel-perfect transparency.

### Step 3: Generate Custom Background / Layers (FLUX.1)
- Click **✨ Generate Layer** in the toolbar.
- Enter your prompt (e.g., *"Luxury dark wooden table with soft neon bokeh"*).
- Select one of the **5 Style Presets**:
  - 📷 **Photorealistic**: Studio product photography.
  - 🎨 **Cyberpunk**: Glowing neon aesthetics.
  - 📊 **E-Commerce**: Clean white/neutral commercial backdrop.
  - 🎭 **Artistic**: Expressive brushstrokes and colors.
  - ✨ **Minimal**: Soft pastel geometric gradients.
- Select your **Aspect Ratio** and click **Generate ✨**.

### Step 4: AI Environmental Relighting (IC-Light)
- Position your transparent subject on top of the generated background.
- Select the foreground node and click **💡 AI Relight**.
- The AI harmonizes shadows, light direction, and ambient bounce between the subject and background.

### Step 5: Undo / Redo and Export
- Use `Ctrl+Z` / `Cmd+Z` to undo any change or AI operation.
- Use `Ctrl+Y` / `Cmd+Y` to redo.
- Click **Export** to save your composite as high-res PNG, JPEG, SVG, or Liquid E-Commerce Template.
